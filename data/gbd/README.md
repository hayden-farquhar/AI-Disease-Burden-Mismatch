# GBD Data

This directory should contain GBD 2023 DALY data downloaded from the IHME GBD Results Tool.

## Required files

### 1. `gbd_2023_global_dalys_number.csv`

Download from: https://vizhub.healthdata.org/gbd-results/

Settings:
- **Measure:** DALYs (Disability-Adjusted Life Years)
- **Metric:** Number
- **Location:** Global
- **Year:** 2021
- **Age:** All ages
- **Sex:** Both
- **Cause:** Level 3 (select all)

### 2. `gbd_2023_sdi_dalys_number.csv` (optional, for equity analysis)

Same settings as above, but:
- **Location:** Low SDI, Low-middle SDI, Middle SDI, High-middle SDI, High SDI

## Citation

GBD 2023 Collaborators. (2025). Burden of 375 diseases and injuries, risk-attributable burden of 88 risk factors, and healthy life expectancy in 204 countries and territories, 1990-2023: a systematic analysis for the Global Burden of Disease Study 2023. *The Lancet*, 406(10513), 1873-1922.
